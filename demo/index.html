Scene

perspective lives on the parent, so every child shares one vanishing point. Put it on the child and a row of rotated cards looks wrong in a way nobody can name.

Stage

preserve-3d goes on the element whose children need depth. Without it they flatten into its plane and translateZ does nothing at all.

Pop

Anything at translateZ is a second depth. One or two per scene is enough. Ten is a fairground, and the reader stops reading.

What this template refuses to do

  • No tilt on touch. The pointer engine checks (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine) before it binds anything.
  • No motion on request. prefers-reduced-motion turns the stage off in both CSS and JS, and the scroll recede never binds.
  • No blurred boxes as halos. A blurred element inset by negative percentages is wider than its parent and gives a phone a horizontal scrollbar. .t3-photo and .t3-halo use a box-shadow, which is never part of the scrollable area.
  • No bleed on a phone. Below 980px the layered hero becomes a column. The photo that leaves the page needs a page wide enough to leave.
  • No backdrop blur above a fixed drawer. backdrop-filter makes its element the containing block for fixed children, which pins a mobile menu to the header instead of the screen.
  • No dependency. Two files, no build step, no network request.
